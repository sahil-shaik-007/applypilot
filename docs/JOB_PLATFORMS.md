# Job Search Platforms Directory

The full list of job sites ApplyPilot can open for you. Type a role (for example *data analyst*) and a location once, and ApplyPilot opens the same search on every site you pick and keeps track of which ones you've used.

**252 platforms** in 11 regions. **144** support one-click deep-link search, and **65** also have a remote-only search.

## How to read this table

- **Search**: a sample search for *data analyst* in a typical city for that region. A dash means the site has no public search link (login required, or the URL can't be built from plain text), so ApplyPilot opens the site's job page instead.
- **Apply**: *Easy apply* means you apply on the platform with a saved profile. *External* means you're sent to the employer's site. *Mixed* means both happen.
- **Free**: whether job seekers can use it without paying. Some free sites also sell optional premium plans.
- **Popularity**: 1 to 5 stars, a rough guide to how many listings and users the site has in its region or niche.

> **Hidden-job tip:** the *ATS search trick* entries run Google searches limited to the hiring systems companies use for their own career pages (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and others). Many of these jobs never show up on the big boards, so fewer people apply to them. Use Google's *Tools > Past week* filter to see only fresh postings.

## Staying safe

- Real employers never charge you for applications, interviews, training or visa processing. If a listing asks for money, leave.
- Don't share bank details or ID documents until you have a written offer you've checked yourself.
- Sites that look like scams, or links that stop working, can be reported from the ApplyPilot feedback menu.
