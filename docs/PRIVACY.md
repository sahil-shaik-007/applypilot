# ApplyPilot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 October 2026_

ApplyPilot is a desktop application that runs entirely on your computer.

**What we collect: nothing.** ApplyPilot has no account system, no analytics and no telemetry. We do not operate servers that receive your profile, resumes, applications, answers or API keys.

**Where your data lives.** Your profile, resumes, job matches, applications, saved answers and settings are stored in your Windows user folder (`%APPDATA%\ApplyPilot`). API keys are encrypted using Windows DPAPI. You can export or delete everything at any time from Settings → Data & privacy, or by uninstalling the app and deleting that folder.

**Who ApplyPilot talks to, and only when you use the feature:**
- **Job sources** (e.g. Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Himalayas, Remote OK, Adzuna). Search terms and location are sent directly from your PC to fetch listings.
- **Employers' application forms.** When you apply, ApplyPilot fills the employer's form in your own browser with the profile details you chose to store. The employer's privacy policy then applies.
- **Anthropic (Claude)**, if you add your own API key. Your resume text, profile summary and the job description are sent to generate cover letters, tailoring or answers, under Anthropic's commercial terms.
- **Dodo Payments**, if you buy a license. Activation sends your license key and a device name. Payment details are handled by Dodo Payments, never by ApplyPilot.

**Children.** ApplyPilot is not intended for anyone under 16.
