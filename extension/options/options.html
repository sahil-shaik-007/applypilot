    Shortcuts

    Alt+Shift+A open · Alt+Shift+S save job · Alt+Shift+F autofill. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts or edge://extensions/shortcuts.

    Privacy

    The extension talks only to the ApplyPilot app on this computer (127.0.0.1), to the web app address you add above, and, when you press Apply in browser, to the job's own site. It reads a page only when you click the extension or use a shortcut. No analytics, no remote code, no ApplyPilot servers. It never submits applications, never fills passwords and never solves CAPTCHAs.
